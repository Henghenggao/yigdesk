# Open-core boundary

Yigdesk is the public adoption slice. Yigrid is the private production product.

## Included under Apache-2.0

- read-only session interface;
- two read-only web components;
- Yigdesk UI and synthetic finance fixture;
- fixed five-formula preview adapter;
- demo consequence-packet shape;
- tests and deployment scaffolding.

## Not included or licensed

- Yigrid general computation kernel or DAG semantics;
- operational-history model or customer op-log data;
- production packet or attestation implementation;
- human-gated commit, authorization, persistent mutation, or write-back;
- vault, signing, transparency-log, compliance, SSO, or multi-tenant services;
- private repositories, roadmaps, customer data, or trade secrets;
- Yigrid or Yigdesk trademarks.

The public components are intentionally domain-neutral. Finance language is isolated to the Yigdesk demo story and its synthetic adapter.
